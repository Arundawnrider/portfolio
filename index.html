About me

I'm a final-year Aeronautical Engineering student at MIT, Chennai, and co-founder of ARIES (Aerobot Research and Innovative Engineering Solutions). My work sits at the intersection of aerospace engineering, embedded systems, and autonomous flight — from CFD simulations of novel UAV configurations to Raspberry Pi-powered drone payloads.

My flagship project, TWiQ, is a tandem-wing hybrid VTOL quadrotor UAV designed for efficient cruise and vertical agility — shaped through ANSYS Fluent CFD, AVL stability analysis, and SolidWorks structural modelling. Alongside engineering design, I build the software that makes drones smarter: real-time geotagged camera systems, Android companion apps, and full-stack flight data tools.

What I'm Doing

  • UAV Design & CFD

    Full-cycle UAV design — wing geometry, boattail fuselage optimisation, SST k-ω CFD in ANSYS Fluent, and AVL longitudinal stability analysis.

  • Embedded & Avionics Systems

    Raspberry Pi payloads, Pixhawk avionics stacks, GPS/UART integration, and real-time sensor fusion for autonomous flight.

  • Drone Software & Apps

    Flask backends, React Native Android apps, GitHub-integrated OTA update systems, and geotagged imaging pipelines for UAV payloads.

  • Aerospace Research

    IEEE-format research on hybrid VTOL aerodynamics, digital twin structural analysis, and drone startup ecosystem development in South India.
