Future of Flight

MIT Chennai

I'm a final-year Aeronautical Engineering student at MIT, Chennai and co-founder of ARIES (Aerobot Research and Innovative Engineering Solutions). My work lives at the intersection of aerospace design, embedded systems, and autonomous flight intelligence.

My flagship project TWiQ is a tandem-wing hybrid VTOL quadrotor UAV shaped through ANSYS Fluent CFD (SST k-ω), AVL stability analysis, and SolidWorks FEA. Alongside hardware, I build the software stack: ARIESCAM, a Raspberry Pi geotagged drone camera system with Flask backend, GPS integration, and an Android companion app.
